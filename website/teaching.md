# Teaching and workshops
{: .no_toc }

Everything you need to teach network analysis with Stata and the nwcommands packages, in one place. The slides are
PowerPoint files with speaker notes (and PDFs); instructors are free to use, adapt and share them.

## Slides

- [Workshop](/book/teaching/workshop/): two sessions of three hours, with slides. Session 1: the
  nwcommands core. Session 2: ERGMs with nwergm and SAOMs with nwsaom. An optional Session 3: relational events
  with nwrem and nwdynam.
- [Course outlines and slides](/book/teaching/outlines/): a 12-week course on descriptive and inferential network analysis,
  a 12-week course on statistical models for networks, and a five-day workshop. Each week or day gives the chapters, the
  core idea, a demonstration with data and commands, and exercises.

## Material for instructors

- [Teaching with the book](/book/teaching/): how the book's chapters are built and how to turn them into class sessions.
- [Notes on every chapter](/book/teaching/chapter-notes/): the exercises of each chapter by level.
- [Assignments](/book/teaching/assignments/): assignments with other data sets.
- [Do-files of the book](/book/dofiles/): every printed command, chapter by chapter, to run in class as they are.

## For students

- [Tutorials](/tutorials/): step-by-step introductions to the core commands, with data and do-files.
- [Installation and setup](/tutorials/01-installation-setup/): what students need on their computers before the first class.
- [Data sets](/datasets/): the example data and how to load them with `nwwebuse`.
- The manuals of the model packages: [nwsaom](/nwsaom/), [nwergm](/nwergm/), [nwrem](/nwrem/manual/) and [nwdynam](/nwdynam/manual/).

The software is free (GNU General Public License, version 3), and Stata/BE, the smallest edition, runs every example.
