# How to cite

**nwlsm.** Cite the package by its repository and report the version that produced your results, as printed by
`nwlsm version` (for example "nwlsm 1.0.6"). To cite the software:

> Grund, T. U. (2026). *nwlsm: a Stata package for latent space models of networks (version 1.0.6).* GitHub. [https://github.com/thomas-u-grund/nwlsm](https://github.com/thomas-u-grund/nwlsm)

Cite the manual, which has a DOI:

> Grund, T. U. (2026). *nwlsm: Latent space models for networks in Stata. Manual.* Zenodo. [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24398243](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24398243)

The DOI is the one of all versions of the manual, so it always leads to the latest manual.

Please also cite the core package, nwcommands.

**The model and the software it follows.** `nwlsm` implements the latent space model as the R package `latentnet` does, so please also cite:

- Hoff, P. D., Raftery, A. E. and Handcock, M. S. (2002). Latent space approaches to social network analysis. *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 97(460), 1090-1098. [doi:10.1198/016214502388618906](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214502388618906)
- Krivitsky, P. N. and Handcock, M. S. (2008). Fitting position latent cluster models for social networks with latentnet. *Journal of Statistical Software* 24(5). [doi:10.18637/jss.v024.i05](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v024.i05)
- Handcock, M. S., Raftery, A. E. and Tantrum, J. M. (2007). Model-based clustering for social networks. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series A* 170(2), 301-354. [doi:10.1111/j.1467-985X.2007.00471.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-985X.2007.00471.x) (for `clusters()`)
- Krivitsky, P. N. and Handcock, M. S. (2025). *latentnet: Latent Position and Cluster Models for Statistical Networks.* R package version 2.12.0, The Statnet Project. [CRAN](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=latentnet)

**The social influence model.** If you use `influence()`, cite:

- Sweet, T. M. and Adhikari, S. (2020). A latent space network model for social influence. *Psychometrika* 85(2), 251-274. [doi:10.1007/s11336-020-09700-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11336-020-09700-x)
