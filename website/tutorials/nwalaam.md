# Autologistic actor attribute models with nwalaam
{: .no_toc }

**The question:** Is a yes/no outcome of actors related to the outcomes of their network partners?

You need the core and the package [nwalaam](/nwalaam/) ([how to install it](/nwalaam/install/)).

- [Tutorial 1: A first ALAAM on the karate club](/nwalaam/tutorial-1-first-alaam/): the estimators side by side, and the exact answer.
- [Tutorial 2: Smoking in the Glasgow friendship network](/nwalaam/tutorial-2-glasgow/): contagion and covariates, goodness of fit, simulation, and what contagion does not say.
