# Exponential random graph models with nwergm
{: .no_toc }

**The question:** Why does one observed network look the way it does?

You need the core and the package [nwergm](/nwergm/) ([how to install it](/nwergm/install/)).

- [Tutorial 1: A first ERGM](/nwergm/tutorial-1-first-ergm/): a first model, information criteria, a Monte Carlo fit and goodness of fit.
- [Tutorial 2: Terms and model specification](/nwergm/tutorial-2-terms/): covariate, structural and triad terms and how to read them.
- [Tutorial 3: Diagnostics and goodness of fit](/nwergm/tutorial-3-diagnostics/): `estat mcmcdiag`, `estat gof` and what to do when a fit does not converge.
- [Tutorial 4: Missing data and comparing models](/nwergm/tutorial-4-missing-comparing/): missing dyads, `estat ic`, `lrtest`, `predict`.
- [Tutorial 5: Constraints, two-mode networks and operators](/nwergm/tutorial-5-constraints-twomode-operators/).
