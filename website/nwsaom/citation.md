# How to cite

**nwsaom.** Cite the nwsaom manual and report the version that produced your results, as printed by
`nwsaom version` (for example "nwsaom 1.0.1"):

> Grund, T. U. (2026). *nwsaom: Stochastic actor-oriented models in Stata. Manual for version 1.0.1.*

The manual (PDF) is in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/thomas-u-grund/nwsaom/blob/main/manual/nwsaom_manual.pdf).
A DOI (Zenodo) will be added. A Stata Journal article is in preparation.

**The model and RSiena.** `nwsaom` implements the model of Snijders and follows RSiena, so please also cite them:

- Snijders, T.A.B. (2001). The statistical evaluation of social network dynamics. *Sociological Methodology* 31, 361-395.
- Ripley, R.M., Snijders, T.A.B., Boda, Z., Voros, A., Preciado, P. *Manual for RSiena.*

Depending on what you used, also cite Lospinoso and Snijders (2019) for goodness of fit, and multiSiena
(Snijders, Koskinen, Ripley) for Bayesian estimation.
