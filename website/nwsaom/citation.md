# How to cite

**nwsaom.** Cite the nwsaom manual and report the version that produced your results, as printed by
`nwsaom version` (for example "nwsaom 1.3.1"):

> Grund, T. U. (2026). *nwsaom: Stochastic actor-oriented models in Stata. Manual for version 1.3.* Zenodo. [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23623807](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23623807)

The DOI is the one of all versions of the manual (there is one Zenodo record, with a new version for each minor release); the label "1.3" is the series and holds for all versions 1.1.x. Always report the exact package version as well.

The manual (PDF) is archived at Zenodo ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.23623807](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23623807)); a copy is in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/thomas-u-grund/nwsaom/blob/main/manual/nwsaom_manual.pdf). A paper on nwsaom is in preparation (see the [literature page](../literature/)).

**The model and RSiena.** `nwsaom` implements the model of Snijders and follows RSiena, so please also cite them:

- Snijders, T.A.B. (2001). The statistical evaluation of social network dynamics. *Sociological Methodology* 31, 361-395.
- Ripley, R.M., Snijders, T.A.B., Boda, Z., Voros, A., Preciado, P. *Manual for RSiena.*

Depending on what you used, also cite Lospinoso and Snijders (2019) for goodness of fit, and multiSiena
(Snijders, Koskinen, Ripley) for Bayesian estimation.
