# News
{: .no_toc }

Announcements about workshops, talks, the book and teaching material, newest first. They also go to the [nwcommands mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/nwcommands). New versions of the software are listed on the [Release notes](../releases/) page, with the current version of every package.

## 22 November 2026: Workshops at Sunbelt 2027 in Montreal

Two workshops are planned for the Sunbelt conference of the International Network for Social Network Analysis (INSNA) in Montreal, Canada: one on network analysis with nwcommands in general, and one on statistical models of networks in Stata (the packages for stochastic actor-oriented, exponential random graph, dynamic network actor, relational event, autologistic actor attribute, latent space and stochastic block models). The conference runs from Monday 21 to Saturday 26 June 2027 at Le Centre Sheraton Montreal Hotel. INSNA schedules the workshops on Monday 21 June (9:00 to 16:00), Tuesday 22 June (9:00 to 16:00) and Wednesday 23 June (9:00 to 12:00), all in person. The workshops are proposed to INSNA; the dates and times will be posted here once INSNA has confirmed and scheduled them. For registration, the programme and all other information, see the [Sunbelt 2027 Montreal website](https://www.insna.org/events/sunbelt-2027-montreal). The [workshop slides](../book/teaching/workshop/) of the three-session workshop are already available.

## 21 November 2026: Manuals updated

The manuals of **nwergm** (series 1.12), **nwrem** (1.7), **nwdynam** (1.11), **nwlsm** and **nwsbm** (series 1.0, with the listings of the current versions) have been updated. The PDFs of the nwdynam and nwrem manuals on this site are the new ones ([nwdynam](../nwdynam/manual/), [nwrem](../nwrem/manual/)); the updated sources are in the repositories of the packages.

## 11 November 2026: The book's do-files

The do-files of *Network Analysis with Stata*, one per chapter, are on the [book's do-file page](../book/dofiles/).
