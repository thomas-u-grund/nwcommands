--- GOTCHA-style notes ---
1. Mata ErgmTermData objects registered with ErgmModel::addterm() must be top-level, permanent variables (the model stores &td0); Stata tempnames created inside a helper program are recycled after it exits and silently overwrite earlier terms. _nwergm_cov.ado names them __nwcv<counter>. Do not pass a td through a second Mata function layer before addterm (the address taken is the layer's parameter).
2. cscript clears Mata: a cscript must "quietly do unw_core.do" and "quietly do unw_ergm.do" after the "cscript" line. A Mata function whose signature uses "class ErgmModel" must be defined AFTER the class (otherwise "ErgmModel() already exists").
3. import delimited makes float variables unless "asdouble" is given: R statistics then differ at 1e-6.
4. Stata max double is 8.98e307: a sentinel of 1e308 is a missing value.
5. R's trust-based curved MPLE is not a maximiser on flat likelihoods; match it, do not "fix" it.
